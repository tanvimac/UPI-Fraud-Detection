Synthetic Data Quality Audit
===================================

1. Fraud label generation logic (from src/data_generator.py):
- Total rows: 10,000, fraud ratio 2% (200 fraud rows).
- Fraud rows receive:
  * Amount: uniform 50,000–200,000 INR (vs normal log‑normal <10,000).
  * transactions_last_1hr: 5–20 (vs Poisson λ=1 for normal).
  * Transaction time: late night (0‑4 am).
  * Device type: chosen from less common ["POS", "ATM"].
  * Merchant category: chosen from rare ["Gifts", "Other"].
  * Other fields (user_id, location, etc.) remain random.

2. Features used by Isolation Forest (src/model_training.py):
   amount, transactions_last_1hr, transaction_hour, transaction_day, is_weekend, is_night, log_amount

6. Assessment:
   - Fraud labels are created by deterministic rules that strongly separate fraud from normal on the engineered numeric features (amount, recent count, night flag, etc.).

   - For a more realistic evaluation, consider adding overlap (e.g., lower fraud amounts, mixed device types) or removing the leakage‑prone features.
